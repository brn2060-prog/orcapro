How did Viking families survive winters with months of darkness, no chimneys, and no way to buy food? This episode follows a Norse farm from the autumn hay count to the midwinter feast, from Norway to Iceland and Greenland, with animated maps showing where the Norse pushed into the cold and why one colony vanished.
